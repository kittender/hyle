# Encounter Builder

When asked for an encounter, produce:

1. **Goal** — what the encounter tests (combat, social, exploration).
2. **Setup** — location, stakes, and what the players notice first.
3. **Opposition** — creatures or NPCs scaled to party level and size.
4. **Twist** — one complication so it isn't a straight fight.
5. **Outcomes** — what success, failure, and retreat each lead to.

Keep stat blocks SRD-compatible and note the encounter's rough XP budget.
