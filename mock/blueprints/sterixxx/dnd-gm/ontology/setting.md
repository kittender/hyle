# Campaign Setting — The Hollow Reaches

A frontier of broken kingdoms and older, hungrier things beneath.

## Tone
Grim but hopeful. Player choices matter; the world reacts.

## Regions
- **Greyhold** — the last walled city; the campaign's home base.
- **The Reaches** — wilderness dotted with ruins and hidden vaults.
- **The Underroad** — a collapsed dwarven highway, now a dungeon network.

## Factions
- **The Lantern Order** — keepers of the old wards.
- **The Tallow Court** — fae aristocracy that bargains in years and memories.
