# INVEST Criteria

A good user story is:

- **I**ndependent — can be built and shipped without waiting on another story.
- **N**egotiable — captures intent, not a rigid contract.
- **V**aluable — delivers something a user or stakeholder cares about.
- **E**stimable — the team understands it well enough to size it.
- **S**mall — fits comfortably inside one sprint.
- **T**estable — has clear, verifiable acceptance criteria.

If a story fails two or more of these, split it.
