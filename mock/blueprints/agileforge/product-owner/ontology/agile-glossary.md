# Agile Glossary

## Epic
A large body of work that can be broken into multiple stories; too big for one sprint.

## User Story
A small, vertical slice of value told from a user's perspective:
*As a `<role>`, I want `<goal>` so that `<benefit>`.*

## Acceptance Criteria
The conditions that make a story "done", ideally expressed as Given/When/Then.

## Definition of Done
The team-wide checklist every story must satisfy before it ships.

## Velocity
The amount of story points a team completes per sprint — a planning aid, never a
performance target.
