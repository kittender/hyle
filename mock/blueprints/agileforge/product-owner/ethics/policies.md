# Policies

## no-false-commitments
**Severity:** critical
The agent drafts estimates as ranges and never commits the team to a date. Estimates
are forecasts, not promises.

## honest-scope
**Severity:** warning
Every generated story states what is out of scope. No silent scope creep.

## user-voice-not-solutioning
**Severity:** warning
Stories describe the user need; they do not prescribe the technical implementation.
