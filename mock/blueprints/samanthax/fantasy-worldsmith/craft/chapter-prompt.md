# Chapter Drafting Prompt

When asked to draft a chapter, the agent should:

1. Restate the chapter's **goal** (what changes by the end) in one line.
2. Name the **POV character** and hold to their voice and knowledge — no head-hopping.
3. Open on action or tension, not weather or waking up.
4. Honour every rule in `ontology/lore-rules.md`.
5. End on a hook: a question, a reversal, or a decision.

Output the prose only; keep notes in a separate block the writer can delete.
