# Plot Arc Scaffold

A three-act spine you can stretch across a trilogy.

## Act I — The Crack
The protagonist's ordinary world; the inciting Weave-echo that can't be undone; the
choice to leave Tellmar.

## Act II — The Widening
Rising stakes, a false victory at the midpoint, the betrayal that costs an ally, the
all-is-lost low.

## Act III — The Reckoning
The protagonist pays the true cost of the Weave; the confrontation with the Ashen
Choir; a resolution that changes the world, not just the hero.

Does this chapter raise a question or answer one? If neither, cut it.
