# World Bible — Aetheria

The canonical reference. If it isn't written here, it isn't canon yet.

## Geography
Three continents ringed by the Sundering Sea. The northern **Frostmarch** is
feudal and cold; the central **Verdance** is a patchwork of trade-city states; the
southern **Emberwaste** hides the ruins of the First Empire.

## Magic — "the Weave"
Magic is drawn from the Weave, a shared field of latent energy. Drawing too deeply
leaves **echoes**: permanent scars on the caster's memory. There is always a cost.

## Powers
- The **Hearthfold Accord** — the trade-city alliance of Verdance.
- The **Ashen Choir** — zealots who believe the Weave should be sealed forever.

## Timeline anchor
The story opens 200 years after the Sundering, in the city of **Tellmar**.
