# Lore Consistency Rules

The agent must enforce these whenever it drafts or edits:

1. **Magic has a cost.** No Weave-working without an echo or a price. No exceptions
   for plot convenience.
2. **Geography is fixed.** Travel times respect the map in the world bible.
3. **No anachronisms.** The setting is pre-industrial; no gunpowder, no printing
   press, unless the bible introduces them deliberately.
4. **Names follow culture.** Frostmarch names are harsh and Norse-flavoured; Verdance
   names are mercantile and Latinate.
5. **Death is permanent** unless a named, costly resurrection rule exists in canon.
