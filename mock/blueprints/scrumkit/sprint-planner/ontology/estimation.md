# Estimation

## Story Points
A relative measure of effort, complexity, and uncertainty — **not** hours. Use a
modified Fibonacci scale: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21.

## Capacity
Realistic availability for the sprint = team-days − holidays − ceremonies − a buffer
for support. Plan to ~80% of raw capacity.

## Sprint Goal
One sentence describing the outcome the sprint is aiming for. If you can't write it,
the sprint isn't focused yet.
