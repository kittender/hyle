# Backlog Refinement Agenda

Timebox: 45–60 min, mid-sprint.

1. **Context (5 min)** — PO frames upcoming priorities.
2. **Walk the top items (25 min)** — clarify, split oversized stories, confirm INVEST.
3. **Acceptance criteria (15 min)** — agree Given/When/Then per story.
4. **Estimate (10 min)** — planning poker; discuss outliers, re-vote once.
5. **Ready check (5 min)** — mark stories that meet the Definition of Ready.

Outcome: top of the backlog is refined two sprints deep.
